What guests feel

Improvement opportunities

Where consistency matters

  • Reduce uncertainty during high-volume arrival periods.
  • Make room-readiness communication more proactive.
  • Create a more intentional post-stay follow-up rhythm.

Priority recommendations

Three actions to move forward

  1. 01
    Establish a consistent arrival-host practice.

    Introduce clear welcome, orientation, and wait-time communication during peak arrival windows.

  2. 02
    Standardize the room-readiness communication path.

    Give guests timely updates and a thoughtful recovery option when rooms are not ready.

  3. 03
    Connect post-stay outreach to relationship building.

    Create a simple follow-up cadence that supports review response and future-stay intent.
